Privacy Policy

Short version: TextFlag runs entirely in your browser. Your lyrics, audio and flags never get sent to me.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy covers TextFlag at ruytha.dev/tools/textflag and its guide. It's run by me, Ruytha, as a personal project, not a company.

Your files TTML lyrics and audio

When you drop in or choose a TTML file or an audio track, your browser reads it on your own device. It isn't uploaded to me or to anyone else. Audio plays from a temporary local link that goes away when you close the tab.

Nothing to upload, nothing to leakTextFlag has no server side. There's no database, no account and no upload endpoint behind it.

What's stored on your device Your browser's local storage

To remember your work between visits, TextFlag saves a few things in your browser's local storage:

  • Your flags for each file, saved under that file's name, so they're still there when you reopen it.
  • Your custom presets: the name and note text for each one.
  • Display settings: text size, wide mode, romaji and line numbers.

This data stays in your browser and I can't see it. It's kept until you clear it. You can delete it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. TextFlag doesn't use cookies.

When you press Export, TextFlag writes your review to your clipboard. It never reads from your clipboard.

Loading from a link Pasting a TTML URL

If you paste a link (or open TextFlag with a ?src= link), your browser downloads the file straight from that website. That website will see the request like any other visit, including your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy. The request doesn't go through me.

Hosting Vercel

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may keep basic request logs (IP address, browser, the page requested and when) to deliver the site and keep it secure. That's handled under Vercel's Privacy Policy.

If you share a TextFlag link on Discord, Discord fetches the page to build its link preview. That's covered by Discord's own policies.

What I don't do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No ads, and I don't sell or share data. There's no data for me to sell anyway.
  • No accounts, sign-ups or emails.

Children

TextFlag isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If TextFlag changes in a way that affects your privacy, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
