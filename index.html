About 自己紹介

An Australian director, editor and lighting artist who makes short films and music videos, and builds the tools to make them.

Most projects run end to end through my hands: animating and rigging in Blender, cutting in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and After Effects, lighting virtual sets and real ones, and shooting stills on a Canon EOS 7D. I write HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, and I'm building VCap Motion, a VR motion capture system for the upcoming YouTube series Borderline Obsession.

I sync and review TTML lyric files for Spicy Lyrics, learn Japanese (がんばります!), and play RDR2 and Forza with Tame Impala on in the background. Say hi on Discord, @ruytha.

            On repeat forever · ずっとリピート中 Tame Impala Tame Impala is really one person: Kevin Parker, an Australian musician who writes, plays, records and produces almost every sound on the albums himself, then tours with a five-piece band. It started as fuzzy psychedelic rock on Innerspeaker and Lonerism, turned into the glossy synth-pop of Currents, and kept going through The Slow Rush and Deadbeat.
