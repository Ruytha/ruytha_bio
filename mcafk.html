How it works

Walk away. Come back to a film.

  1. 01

    Go idle

    After 60 seconds with no input (you can change this), the camera takes over. Or press F8 to roll right away.

  2. 02

    Roll camera

    The HUD hides, bars slide in, your player starts an idle animation and the director cuts to a new shot every 12 seconds.

  3. 03

    Cut

    Move the mouse, press a key or take damage and you're back in first person. That key press won't also swing or jump.

Controls

Two keys, both rebindable in Options → Controls.

F8Start or stop the cinematic right now.
F7Open the settings, with a live letterbox preview.
AnyAny key or mouse movement ends the shot.

Shot list

Five camera moves. Turn each on or off, and play them in order or shuffled. Diagrams show the camera path around you.

  • Orbit

    A slow circle around you.

  • PTZ

    A mounted camera pans, tilts and zooms in. Sometimes it pulls out instead.

  • Dolly in

    Pushes in from a wide shot to a close-up.

  • Crane up

    Starts low at your feet and rises high above you.

  • Tracking

    Slides sideways past you, keeping you in frame.

Idle animations New

Your player doesn't just stand there. Pick one, or shuffle between them on every cut.

  • Sitting

    Sits on the ground, leans back on its hands and looks around.

  • Sword play

    A wind-up and slash, then twirls the blade in a guard stance.

  • Pickaxe

    Taps at the ground a few times, then inspects the pick.

  • Look around

    Glances left and right, and now and then has a big stretch.

  • Wave

    Every so often, waves at the camera.

Sword and pickaxe put a prop in your hand if you aren't holding one. Animations only show on your screen.

Install

Client-side only, so it works on any server.

  1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.20.1.
  2. Put Fabric API in your mods folder.
  3. Put afk-cine-cam-1.0.0.jar in the same folder.
  4. Launch, stand still and wait a minute.
