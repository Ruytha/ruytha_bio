Shutter.

Frame-accurate review notes for film and TV. Park on a frame, pin it, box it or draw on it, then hand your editor a marker list that drops straight into Resolve.

Start a reviewVisit shutter.ruytha.dev

From playback to a marker list in four moves.

  1. Open

    Any clip, no upload

    MP4, MOV, WebM or a still. It plays straight from your drive, whatever the file size.

  2. Park

    Land on the exact frame

    Step with the arrow keys, jump a second with Shift. Timecode reads HH:MM:SS:FF from your edit's start TC.

  3. Mark

    Pin, box or draw

    Tag each note Color, Framing, Focus, VFX or Edit so the right person picks it up.

  4. Hand off

    Files, not screenshots

    A frame grab with marks burned in, a CSV, or an EDL of timeline markers for Resolve.

Your footage stays on your computer.

Shutter reads the file in your browser and plays it from there. Notes sync to your account so a review started at your desk is waiting on your phone. The footage never goes with them.

  • Computer, iPad or phoneReviews run in the browser on all three.
  • Works like an appAdd it to your home screen. Clips already on the device review offline.
  • No upload by defaultFootage only leaves your machine if you choose to upload on a paid plan.
