How a project runs.

Four steps, one point of contact the whole way through.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You share the idea, the song or the story, and any references.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree on the look, the shots and what gets delivered.

  3. 03

    Make

    Direction, lighting and the edit, with check-ins along the way.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final files in the formats you need, ready to post.

Software

Built in‑house.

Shutter is the studio's review app: frame-accurate notes for film and TV, exported as markers that drop straight into Resolve. Slate plans the shoot, Cuelight lights the show in Blender, and Tools does the maths.
