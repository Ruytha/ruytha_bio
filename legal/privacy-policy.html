Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version

  • There are no visitor accounts, no analytics and no ads, and I don't sell anything about you.
  • The only personal info I keep is what you give me for a quote page: your username, profile picture and quote.
  • Games and tools save your progress in your own browser. I can't see it.
  • A few outside services (Vercel, Ko-fi, Google Fonts, Last.fm) help run the site and have their own policies.

1Who I am

ruytha.dev (“the Site”) is a personal website run by me, Ruytha, an individual in Australia. It's not a company. This policy covers every page on the Site, including the quote pages and the boykisser clicker.

TextFlag has its own, more detailed TextFlag Privacy Policy. It works the same way: everything stays in your browser.

2Quote pages

If you get a quote page on the Site, I collect and publish:

  • your username
  • your profile picture
  • your quote

That's used only to build your quote page, which anyone can see. It stays up while your quote page is active (see the Terms of Service). Once a quote page expires, I may take it down and delete what was on it. You can ask me to remove it sooner at any time.

3What's saved in your browser

Some parts of the Site remember things using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to me:

  • Boykisser clicker: your kisses, upgrades and stats, so your progress is still there next time.
  • TextFlag: your flags, presets and display settings.

You can clear it at any time by deleting this site's data in your browser settings, or with “wipe save” in the clicker. The public site doesn't set any cookies.

The admin pageThe admin page is only for me to update the Site. It uses Clerk for sign-in, which uses its own cookies. Visitors never need to sign in.

4Outside services

When you load a page, your browser also connects to a few other services. Each of them can see normal connection info, like your IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy:

  • Vercel hosts the Site and may keep basic request logs to deliver and protect it. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts serves the site's font. Google Privacy Policy
  • Ko-fi serves the “Support this website” button on the home page. Ko-fi Privacy Policy
  • Last.fm provides my “Recent Streams” list and its album art. It shows what I've been listening to, not you. Last.fm Privacy Policy

Links to other sites (Discord, Spotify, Spicy Lyrics, my blog and others) take you somewhere I don't control, so their own policies apply there. If you share a link to the Site on Discord, Discord fetches the page to build its preview.

5Payments and donations

Donations and quote page purchases or renewals go through Ko-fi. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle the payment, and I never see or store your card or bank details. I only see what Ko-fi shows me, like your name, any message you leave and the amount.

6What I don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or ads.
  • No selling, renting or trading information about anyone.
  • No mailing list, and no emails you didn't ask for.

7Removing or checking your info

You can ask me at any time what I have about you, to fix it, or to take it down. That's usually just your quote page. Message me (details below) and I'll sort it out as soon as I can.

8Children

The Site isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal info from them. If you think a child has sent me their info for a quote page, let me know and I'll remove it.

9Changes

If the way the Site handles information changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

10Contact

Message @ruytha on Discord, or ask in my Discord server.
