Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This website (ruytha.dev, "the Site") is run by me personally — it is not a company or business. This Privacy Policy explains what information I collect from you and how I use it if you get a quote page featured on the Site.

1. What I Collect

If you submit a quote to be featured on the Site, I collect:

  • Your username
  • Your profile picture
  • Your quote (the text you want displayed)

This information is used only to display your quote page on the Site.

2. How Long I Keep It

Your username, profile picture, and quote are kept forever for as long as your quote page stays active — I don't automatically delete this information. It's displayed publicly on your quote page.

See the Terms of Service for how long a quote page stays active and how to renew it.

3. Funding (Ko-fi)

This Site is kept online through donations and quote page purchases/renewals made via Ko-fi. I don't run ads or sell your information to anyone. If you pay through Ko-fi, that payment is handled entirely by Ko-fi under its own Privacy Policy — I never see or store your payment details, only what Ko-fi shares with me (like your username/message).

4. Custom Domain Notice

If the Site is reachable via a custom quote/vanity domain, that custom domain is only guaranteed to stay active for 12 months. After that, it may stop working and the Site may only be reachable at its default URL.

5. Removing Your Information

If you want your username, profile picture, or quote taken down, just contact me using the details on the Site and I'll remove it. Otherwise it stays up for as long as your quote page is active.

6. Third-Party Services

The Site relies on third-party services (hosting, domain registrar, Ko-fi) to run. Each has its own privacy practices outside of my control — I'd recommend checking their policies too.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy if how the Site works changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out using the contact details listed on the Site.

This Privacy Policy is for a small, personal website run by an individual, and isn't a substitute for legal advice.
